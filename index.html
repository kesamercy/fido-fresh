The business case for becoming the hub

Dog owners buy food once a month. But they need a vet, walker, groomer, and sitter every single week. By owning the directory, Fido Fresh becomes the brand they associate with caring for their dog — not just feeding them.

Trust compounds. Every useful search builds brand equity that converts at the food purchase moment.
